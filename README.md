### About Me

I operate at the intersection of **Data Science, Quantitative Modeling, and AI Engineering**, translating mathematical foundations and frontier machine learning research into analytical pipelines and scalable production software.

- **Financial Markets & Analytics:** Currently working as a Data Analyst at **Itaú Unibanco**. Previously built quantitative analysis pipelines for **High-Frequency Trading (HFT)** covering data cleaning, feature engineering, and univariate/multivariate time-series modeling.
- **Applied AI & Software Engineering:** Full-stack engineering of production AI systems, architecting autonomous AI agents, RAG workflows, relational/NoSQL databases, and web applications built for operational efficiency.
- **Scientific Research (FAPESP & CNPq):** Funded research in **Explainable AI (XAI)**, Feature Space Visualization for Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), and Distributed Machine Learning under the *Travelling Model* paradigm.
- **Languages:** Portuguese (Native) &bull; English (Cambridge C2 Proficiency) &bull; Spanish (Native/Bilingual).
